# 0014. Clock chip: on-board PCF85063

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-02

## Decision

On-board PCF85063 on I2C 13/14; its interrupt on GPIO15 wakes the ESP32 for alarms. NTP corrects it daily.

## Alternatives rejected

RV-3028; DS3231 module.

## Rationale

Already on the board, low draw, and the interrupt line is wired to an RTC-capable GPIO.
