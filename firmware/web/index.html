    WAV, 16-bit, mono, 16 to 48 kHz, up to 1.5 MB (about 35 s at 22 kHz). The clock loops it while the alarm rings and beeps instead if the file is missing. To convert in Audacity: Tracks > Mix > Mix Stereo Down to Mono, then File > Export > Export as WAV, 16-bit PCM. With ffmpeg: ffmpeg -i in.mp3 -ac 1 -ar 22050 -sample_fmt s16 out.wav
